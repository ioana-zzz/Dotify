## Dotify

**Dotify** is a full-stack music streaming web application inspired by platforms like Spotify.  
It allows users to discover, search, and listen to songs and albums, while artists can upload, manage, and edit their own music content.

### Features

- **User Authentication:** Register, log in, and manage your profile.
- **User Dashboard:**  
  - View and play your liked songs.
  - Search for songs and albums by name.
  - Browse search results and listen instantly.
- **Artist Dashboard:**  
  - Upload new songs and albums.
  - Edit album details and cover art.
  - Delete songs or entire albums.
  - View all your uploaded content in one place.
- **Music Player:**  
  - Stream songs directly in the browser.
  - View album cover art and artist information.
- **Like Songs:**  
  - Users can like/unlike songs and see their liked songs in their dashboard.
- **Responsive Design:**  
  - Clean, modern UI that works on desktop and mobile.

### Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Vite, CSS Modules
- **Backend:** NestJS, TypeORM, PostgreSQL
- **File Handling:** Songs and album covers are stored as base64

### How it Works

- **Users** can search for music, like songs, and manage their profile.
- **Artists** can upload, edit, and delete their own songs and albums.
- All users can stream music and view album/artist details.
